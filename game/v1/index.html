Star Fighter

Rules

  1. There are two players. Player to go first will be randomly selected.
  2. The first player rolls two dice, and the sum of the rolls will be the damage dealt to the opponent... unless either roll is a 1.
  3. If either roll is a 1, the player that rolled will take damage instead with the sum of the rolls times a multiplier. Then, the turn will end and the other player will take their turn.
  4. If both rolls are 1, the player that rolled will take damage for the accumulated damage that they have dealt to the opponent this turn.
  5. The player can choose to roll again until they lose their turn, or choose to end their turn.
  6. When a player chooses to end a turn, they will roll the dice and gets healed the sum of the rolls times the multiplier. This will not occur when losing a turn by rolling a 1.
  7. Once a player's turn ends, the other player's turn will start.
  8. The multiplier starts at 1, and increases every other time a player rolls the dice. The multiplier resets every time a player starts a new turn.
  9. The player that gets their opponent to 0 health wins the game!
